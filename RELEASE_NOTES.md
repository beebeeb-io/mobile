# Beebeeb 1.0.1

This update improves photo backup, uploads, sharing, and vault recovery on iOS.

- Fixes a startup crash in recent builds and prevents duplicate photo backups. Backup counts update when you turn backup off and on.
- Shows storage locations by country and displays the selected location during uploads. Unavailable locations are explained clearly.
- Catches up with file changes after reconnecting and respects the server's retry delay when uploads are busy.
- Keeps new uploads visible and video controls accessible. Closing a video preview cancels its background work.
- Pauses background sync and backup checks while the app is locked or in the background.
- Shares use one complete encrypted link. Sign-in errors are clearer, and password-reset recovery explains when your 12-word recovery phrase is needed.
