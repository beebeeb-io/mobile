Beebeeb is encrypted cloud storage that actually keeps your files private.

Every file, every photo, every folder name is encrypted on your device before it ever leaves it. We can't read your files. We can't recover them if you lose your password. That's the point.

PRIVACY THAT DOES NOT NEGOTIATE

The way most cloud storage works: they hold the keys. They can read your files. Their employees can read your files. Their government can subpoena your files. A breach exposes your files.

The way Beebeeb works: you hold the keys. We store encrypted ciphertext we cannot decrypt. A breach reveals nothing useful. A subpoena gets nothing useful. We designed it this way on purpose.

If you forget your password, we cannot recover it. We will not pretend otherwise.
